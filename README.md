# AI Dayə — Childcare Marketplace

[![Live demo](https://img.shields.io/badge/Live_demo-000000?style=for-the-badge&logo=vercel&logoColor=white)](https://aidaye.vercel.app)

A childcare marketplace prototype designed to make caregiver discovery, pricing and booking clearer for families.

## Product challenge

Finding childcare involves trust, availability, pricing and agreement decisions. The product brings these steps into one structured journey instead of leaving families to coordinate everything manually.

## Key capabilities

- Caregiver discovery and availability filtering
- Child count, service duration and care-needs pricing logic
- Transparent caregiver earnings and platform-fee calculation
- Booking and urgent-request prototype flows
- Digital agreement/PDF generation concept
- Responsive Azerbaijani-first interface

## Technology

`HTML` `CSS` `JavaScript` `Node.js` `Express`

## Interview walkthrough

Change the number of children, service duration and care requirements, then review how matching and pricing respond. The agreement flow demonstrates how the booking journey could continue after selection.

> Portfolio prototype. Identity verification, legal and payment integrations are represented as product concepts and are not production services.
